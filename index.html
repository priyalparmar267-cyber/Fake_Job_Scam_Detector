🛡️ Safety Recommendations

  • Never pay money to get a job.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.
  • Verify the company before accepting an offer.
  • Be careful with urgent job offers.
  • Do not click suspicious links.

ℹ️ About This Project

The Fake Job / Recruitment Scam Detector is a web-based application designed to help users identify common warning signs in suspicious recruitment messages.

The system analyzes the message for patterns such as payment requests, requests for sensitive information, urgency, unrealistic salary promises, instant selection claims, and suspicious communication channels.

Based on the detected warning signs, the application calculates a risk score from 0 to 100 and displays a risk level to help users make safer decisions before responding to a recruitment message.

🔍 Rule-Based Analysis Checks common recruitment scam patterns.
📊 Risk Score Provides an easy-to-understand score from 0–100.
🛡️ Safety Guidance Provides recommendations for safer online recruitment.
